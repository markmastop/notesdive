# NotesDive 0.18.9 — Reorder slides

Built 24 October 2026 for Apple Silicon macOS.

- In Edit slide, use Move earlier or Move later to move the selected slide one position in the presentation.
- The slide stays selected after moving. Its Markdown, scoped directives, images and speaker notes travel together.
- Undo slide change restores the latest move, insertion or deletion while the source is otherwise unchanged. Further text edits disable that single undo step.
- Presentation settings remain at the start of the Markdown file. Moving the final slide also preserves valid slide separators when it has no final newline.

Existing insertion, deletion, preview and automatic saving continue to work. Shared Marp directives follow source order; scoped directives stay attached to their slide.

This download supports Apple Silicon macOS only. The app uses an ad-hoc signature and is not notarized. Existing PDF, presentation and reMarkable preview/export limitations continue to apply.
